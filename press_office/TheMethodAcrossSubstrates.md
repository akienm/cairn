# The method across substrates

*Draft, awaiting the signature gate. A concept-piece: the prose is the implementation, and
its seal is a human reviewer quorum. Ticket 907f456a6136. Cites
[`FactSheet.md`](FactSheet.md) §9 for its one measurement and
[`PatternWorkflowAndArtifacts.md`](PatternWorkflowAndArtifacts.md) §2.4 for the mechanism;
it restates neither.*

## 1. The measured failure

The twelve pattern pieces each open with a failure that happened. Read together
([FactSheet §9](FactSheet.md), one reader, n=1, a hypothesis to sharpen), they name 33
failures: 21 stated as a property of the system, 9 as a property of the mind doing the work,
3 as both. The twelve that touch the mind are things like a model that does not add logging
until told, a diagnostic read so often it stopped being read, a plan believed for four days
beside a label nobody read.

None of the twelve pieces says so in those words. Each states its cure as a property of the
system: a gate, an address, a deposit. The cure is a structure standing in for something the
reasoner cannot reliably supply on its own.

## 2. The pattern

One optimization at a time. Find a place where a mind does a thing unreliably, measure it,
and move the thing out of the mind and into a structure that does not depend on the mind
remembering. The structure is then proved, and only then relied on
([PatternWorkflowAndArtifacts §2.4](PatternWorkflowAndArtifacts.md)).

The method is not specific to its first subject. It was applied to one kind of mind over a
long time, finding by finding. It was then applied to a second mind of a different kind, a
language model, and the findings that transferred were the ones about conditions, not about
makeup: what a reasoner does when the answer is near, when the context is gone, when a
label is plausible, when nothing forces the why. Those do not depend on what the reasoner
is made of.

The frame is already in the roots. Law 5 calls it *designing to the tool*: the reader is
Claude, and a rule demanding a reader nobody is serves itself. CP4 names the affected beings
the system answers to.

## 3. How it is enforced

By the same means as every other pattern here. A finding is not kept as advice; it becomes a
sieve, a schema field or a door (Law 4). A finding that has not yet become one is a tracked
debt, not a resting state.

## 4. What it costs

Each optimization is small and each one needs a measurement first, so the method is slow and
cannot be done in one insight. It also needs the subject to be measurable. Law 10 holds that
everything here is, except the reasoner, for now. Findings about the reasoner are therefore
records of observation at n=1, and are labeled so.

## 5. What would falsify this

- A reader outside Cairn cannot restate the mechanism after reading this, or cannot name
  one finding they would apply to their own work.
- Readers in cognitive architecture report that nothing here transfers to their setting.
  Then the piece is retired, by Akien's signature; the drafter deletes nothing.

## 6. What is built, and what is red

Built: the twelve pattern pieces, and the one measurement in FactSheet §9. Red: the §9
count is one reader's reading and has no second reader; the classification of a failure as
"of the mind" is a judgment, not an instrument; the transfer claim has had no outside reader
yet, and the seal is the human quorum that would supply the first.
